# Steering Committee Policies

This directory holds written policies adopted by the Steering Committee of the Reciprocal Space Station Consortium under Article 4.3.H of the [Charter](../RSS-charter.md).

Steering Committee policies are binding and direct the work of the Consortium on matters within the Committee's authority — including release and publication, contributions and intellectual property, funding and sponsorship arrangements, and project approval. All policies here must be consistent with the Charter; in the event of conflict, the Charter controls.

Policies are adopted, amended, and repealed by majority vote of the Steering Committee (Article 4.4).
